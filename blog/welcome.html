Welcome — What This Blog Is For

Iqbal K I · August 2026 · 3 min read

I'm starting this blog for a simple reason: most of what I actually learn while researching deep reinforcement learning for portfolio management never makes it into a paper. Papers report results — the final model, the final metric, the clean story. They don't have room for the three approaches that failed first, the dataset quirk that cost a week, or the intuition that only clicked after staring at a reward curve for too long.

This blog is where that goes.

What to expect

Roughly, three kinds of posts:

  • Research notes — plain-language walkthroughs of ideas from my published work and works in progress, written for readers who know finance or ML but not necessarily both.
  • Market intuition — short pieces connecting what an agent learns in simulation to how markets actually behave.
  • Field notes — the practical, unglamorous side of doctoral research: tooling, data pipelines, and the occasional rant about backtesting pitfalls.

If you're a fellow researcher, a student exploring quantitative finance, or just someone curious about what happens when you point a reinforcement learning agent at a stock market, this is for you.

The goal isn't to make the work sound simpler than it is — it's to make it legible.

You can browse the full list of publications on the Research page, or start with the first proper post: Why Deep Reinforcement Learning Might Change Portfolio Management.
